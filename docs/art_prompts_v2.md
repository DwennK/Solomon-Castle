High-end hand painted pre-rendered game sprites with sculpted volume, strong readable silhouettes at 100 pixels height, expressive stylized adult proportions (not chibi), rich cloth and metal. Elevated 3/4 overhead camera, characters facing slightly right and toward viewer. Ivory, deep midnight teal, antique gold, jewel magic, warm upper left key and cold cyan rim light. Row 3: floating spectral woman with flowing torn pale cyan robes; crowned skeletal king with bronze armor and halberd; massive green plague abomination; large obsidian demon with folded burning wings. Row 4: sinister lich archmage with elaborate purple and gold robes and suspended skulls; stout friendly merchant in russet coat with backpack; elder mage teacher in ochre robes holding book; serene healer in sage-green robes with lantern. Coherent exquisite painted rendering, face and weapon visible, every silhouette clearly separate, no ground shadows outside characters, no glow spilling beyond cell. All 16 complete full-body figures, nothing cropped.
